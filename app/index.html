Company profile

What they sell: compliance and repairs software for UK housing associations. It covers gas safety, damp and mould, and repairs scheduling.

Customers: 200 housing associations. Average recurring spend is about £18k a year. The 3 largest customers make up 14% of recurring revenue.

People: 60 staff: R&D 20, professional services 16, support 10, sales and marketing 6, G&A 8.

History: founded in 2009 by Dave Pryce, who stays on as CEO. Acquired by the portfolio in Nov 2026.
